About

A bit about me

I was born in India, stayed there for maybe 3 months, then made the move and grew up in Canada. Moved to the US in June 2025 because we couldn't get enough. Three countries later and I still can't figure out which one I like the most. I guess I'll just say I'm from everywhere.

I got into tech because I kept wondering what was actually going on inside things. At the ripe age of 8, I started tinkering and coding in basic python and html after my grandpa gave me my first laptop. It was nothing much but a crappy celeron but at that time it was pretty powerful.

When I'm not at my desk I'm probably outside touching grass, or reading about some cool github projects I could never make. Or staring at a half-soldered board trying to figure out what went wrong.
